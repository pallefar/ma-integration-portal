<objective>
Deliver the client half of SYN-03: a gated stage control (pill plus caret menu) that replaces the
click-to-cycle-and-wrap button, and a baseline history panel with the deal case pinned at the top
and a role-gated re-baseline control (D-08, D-09, D-12, D-14, D-15, UI-SPEC §1 and §3).

Purpose: the server gate exists after Plan 03, but a control that wraps from the final stage back to
the first is how a silent demotion happens, and a baseline list nobody can see is not history.
Output: two rewritten regions of `renderSynergy()` plus their page-local CSS.

Note on the authz split (D-15, DD-04 precedent): the button's conditional rendering here is the
weaker, cosmetic half. The route's own refusal is the control. Both exist; neither substitutes for
the other.
</objective>
