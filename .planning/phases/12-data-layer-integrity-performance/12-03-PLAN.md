## Planner decisions (this plan)
- **PD-17** The stamp is the first 16 hex characters of sha256 over the exact bytes served at that URL — transitive for
  JS/CSS that themselves reference stamped assets — so "stamp == hash" is checkable from the outside by fetching both.
  Hand-written stamps stay in the source files (no mass edit); they are overwritten at serve time and become the
  negative control (rewrite removed ⇒ test fails). Inline `?v=`-stamped references inside HTML text are rewritten too.
