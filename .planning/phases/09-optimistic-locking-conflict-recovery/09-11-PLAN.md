<objective>
Fix R7 F-2 at its root, inside the shared fetch wrapper: the caller's fetch promise is held while the
conflict dialog is open and resolved with whatever the user's decision produced — the reapplied
response, the DELETE response, or the original 409. Every existing page handler then does the right
thing with no page change (D-05), DD's unconditional `.then(refresh)` waits until the dialog has
settled instead of re-rendering underneath it, and the session model gives defined behaviour for every
probe edge (queue ordering, double clicks, network failure, third writer).

Purpose: SC5 — "After 'Apply my changes anyway' or 'Delete anyway' the page shows the server's
resulting state — no false self-conflict, no deleted row left on screen, no DD refresh under the open
dialog".
Output: held-promise conflict sessions in transitions.js, proven in node:vm and in a real browser.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-09-46 | Denial of Service | unbounded queue of held conflict promises | medium | mitigate | FIFO cap 20; overflow resolves immediately with its own 409 and renders nothing (HP10) |
| T-09-47 | Information Disclosure / Tampering | a held promise resolving with another request's response | high | mitigate | Each session holds only its own resolve; settle is per-session and once-only; identity assertions in HP2/HP3/HP9/HP11 |
| T-09-48 | Tampering | wrapper auto-resubmitting a conflicted write | high | mitigate | Re-fire only from an explicit Apply/Delete-anyway click; in-flight guard prevents duplicates (HP8); the "no retry" case stays green (D-05/Pitfall 11) |
| T-09-50 | Tampering (XSS) | dialog re-render from fresh 409 bodies | medium | mitigate | textContent-only construction unchanged; test/client-xss-sinks.test.js green |
</threat_model>
