<objective>
Deliver ORG-02: the Integration Lead records a role-selection or redundancy decision against a
position, in an append-only log whose latest entry is the position's derived current decision (D-13),
using a fixed five-value vocabulary (D-14), behind a write gate that admits only `admin` and the
Integration Lead (D-04).

Purpose: "role selection" is the act that makes a target org design real, and it is the most
consequential thing this app will ever record about a named individual. It is also the surface where
an authz defect would matter most — one has surfaced at a review or verification gate in five
consecutive phases on this project, which is why D-04 restates the rule explicitly rather than leaving
it to inference.
Output: a restricted decision register with a three-copy vocabulary and an anti-drift test, a derived
current-decision value that is never stored, and a decision badge plus history panel on the tree.

**FLAGGED PLANNER ASSUMPTION — the one `unclassified` edge the probe surfaced, unresolved.** What is
the state of a decision entry whose `positionId` no longer exists — the position was deleted, or the
occupant was unlinked after the decision was recorded? Nothing in 06-CONTEXT.md, the ROADMAP or the
UI-SPEC decides it, and the edge-probe protocol forbids silently backstopping or dismissing an
`unclassified` row. This plan assumes, and states in code comments and in `must_haves`: the log
**retains the orphaned entry verbatim**, because append-only is absolute and destroying a governance
record because its subject was reorganised would be the worst possible failure mode for a legal
artifact; and the **derivation ignores it**, so a deleted position contributes nothing to a current
decision or to Plan 07's rollup counts. Surfaced for the developer, not silently chosen.

**Planner discretion recorded (delegated by 06-CONTEXT.md — i18n key naming):** the five values map to
`org.dec_retain`, `org.dec_redeploy`, `org.dec_in_selection`, `org.dec_redundant` and `org.dec_open`.
The hyphenated value becomes an underscore in its key, because the client builds the key by
concatenation exactly as the RAID severity badge does. English text ships inline on the badge in this
plan and relies on the documented fall-back-to-English contract; the DE/ZH/CS translations land in
Plan 08 alongside the demo seed.

**Planner discretion recorded (delegated by 06-RESEARCH.md Open Question 1):** the vocabulary gets a
client-side JS array copy in `public/js/org.js`, not five static option tags. D-14 is a BRAND-NEW enum,
which puts it in the three-copy `SYN_STAGES` situation rather than the two-copy reuse situation, and a
client array is what gives the anti-drift test a third thing to assert against.

Canon-referral note (prohibition probe): injection through the free-text `rationale`, and GDPR/erasure
obligations over a governance log naming individuals, are canon — covered by VAL-02's write-time
sanitization, the `textContent` render discipline and `/gsd-secure-phase`. Not minted as bespoke
prohibitions here. The two prohibitions this plan does carry are bespoke to a system that records
redundancy decisions against named people, and no other tool would catch them.
</objective>
