<objective>
Extend the proven Option-B mechanism from the tracer's single path to the rest of D-09's scope: the audit append and the control-plane direct-SQL writers that bypass `writeDb()` entirely.

Purpose: 08-RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 found that `db/controlStore.js`'s read-modify-write functions issue their SELECT and their UPDATE as two separate autocommit statements — a silent last-writer-wins overwrite that never even throws `SQLITE_BUSY`, because the race is between two statements rather than at the lock. Deal membership and DD clearance grants are MNPI access-control data; a silently lost grant change is an integrity failure, not just a correctness bug. The researcher recommended closing it in Phase 8; this plan takes that recommendation explicitly rather than inheriting a gap by omission.
Output: `.immediate` lock acquisition across the audit and control-plane writers, with the audit no-swallow contract intact under contention, and a multi-process test proving two concurrent control-plane edits both survive.
</objective>
