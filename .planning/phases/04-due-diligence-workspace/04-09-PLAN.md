<objective>
Close G-04-10: a wizard step the operator SKIPPED is being recorded as DONE.

`public/js/deal-wizard.js`'s Finish control posts `{ step: 'ddKickoff', complete: true }`,
conflating two different intents — "mark the whole setup complete" and "mark the
ddKickoff step done". Live evidence from the UAT (test 10): after skipping steps
4, 5 and 6, the deal record held `setup.steps.scope = null` and `timeline = null`
(correct) but `ddKickoff = {at: <the moment Finish was clicked>, by: karen}`
(wrong). The deal home therefore showed "Data Room & DD Kickoff: DONE" for a step
that was never done, and the Integration Lead is never nudged to finish it.

This plan separates the two intents at the source of truth: the setup route learns
to accept a completion-only post that stamps no step at all, and the Finish control
sends exactly that.

Purpose: the deal-home setup checklist must report outstanding work honestly. It is
also a hard prerequisite for 04-10-PLAN.md, whose wizard resume cursor reads
`setup.steps` to decide which step to resume at — a falsely-stamped step would send
a resuming operator straight past work they never did.

Output: an amended `DealSetupSchema`, a completion-only branch in the setup route, a
Finish control that stamps no step, and four regression tests (three live-HTTP, one
source) that fail against today's code.
</objective>
