| admin upload -> deal slide store | untrusted markup rendered to every member who opens the slide |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-14-40 | Elevation of Privilege | deal slide script acting with the viewer's session | high | mitigate | SLIDE_SANDBOX_CSP on the route + sandboxed iframe; amended slideSandbox real-lane probes under the enforced policy |
| T-14-41 | Information Disclosure | deal slides in a per-instance/shared directory | high | mitigate | deal lane writes only to `<id>.files/slides/`; tracer asserts the demo dir unchanged |

| T-14-44 | Denial of Service | deleted slides never freeing quota | low | mitigate | unreferenced deal file unlinked on last record delete; shared-url row |
